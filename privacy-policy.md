## 1. Introduction

UNBUNNED ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
When you use UNBUNNED, you may voluntarily provide:
- **Dietary preferences and filters** (e.g. food types you wish to exclude)
- **Nutritional goals** (daily calorie target, macro targets)
- **Health goals** (e.g. weight loss, muscle gain)
- **Saved meals and comparisons**

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device information**: device type, operating system version, and app version, used solely for technical support and crash diagnosis
- **Usage data**: general app usage patterns (e.g. features accessed), collected in aggregate and not linked to any individual

## 3. How We Store Your Data

All personal preferences, saved meals, and nutritional targets you set in UNBUNNED are stored **locally on your device** using your device's secure storage. This data is not transmitted to our servers or any third party.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.

For information on how Apple handles your payment data, please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
